07 / HELP DESK

FREQUENTLY
ASKED

WHO CAN PARTICIPATE?+

Beginner's Paradise is for ages 13–21. Beginners are especially welcome.

DO I NEED CODING EXPERIENCE?+

No. Come ready to learn, experiment, and ask questions as you build.

CAN I WORK WITH A TEAM?+

Yes — you can build solo or collaborate with a group.

WHAT CAN I BUILD?+

Websites, apps, games, AI projects, creative experiments, hardware, or something entirely new.

CAN I USE AI?+

Yes. AI tools are allowed, but you should genuinely understand and contribute to your project.

HOW ARE PROJECTS JUDGED?+

Projects are recognized across creativity, execution, technical achievement, design, learning, and polish.

DO I NEED GITHUB?+

Not necessarily. Use the tools that help you build and clearly share what you made.

WHAT DO I SUBMIT?+

Submit your project along with a clear explanation of what you built and what you learned.

HOW DO THE .XYZ PRIZES WORK?+

Winners receive a free .xyz domain for one year and an award certificate. Renewal after the first year is the domain owner’s responsibility.
